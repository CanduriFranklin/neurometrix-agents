# Perfusion Thresholds — CT Perfusion (CTP)

## TMax (Time to Maximum of the residue function)
- Normal: < 4 seconds
- Penumbra (tissue at risk): 4 – 6 seconds
- Severe hypoperfusion: > 6 seconds
- Ischemic core (with reduced CBV): > 10 seconds
- Source: DEFUSE 2 (Lansberg 2012), EXTEND-IA (Campbell 2015)

## CBF (Cerebral Blood Flow)
- Normal: 40 – 60 mL/100g/min
- Penumbra: 20 – 40 mL/100g/min
- Ischemic core: < 20 mL/100g/min
- Source: Wintermark 2005, Koenig 1998

## CBV (Cerebral Blood Volume)
- Normal: 3 – 5 mL/100g
- Penumbra: > 2.0 mL/100g (intact autoregulation)
- Core: < 2.0 mL/100g (> 30% reduction vs contralateral)
- Source: Schaefer 2008, Campbell 2011

## MTT (Mean Transit Time)
- Normal: 4 – 6 seconds
- Prolonged: > 6.5 seconds
- Source: Ostergaard 1996 (central volume theorem)

## Mismatch core/penumbra
- Definition: penumbra volume / core volume
- Reperfusion target: mismatch ratio > 1.8, core < 70 mL
- Source: DEFUSE 2

## Central volume theorem
MTT = CBV / CBF (with hematocrit and tissue density correction)

## Physical corrections
- Tissue density rho = 1.04 g/mL (average brain density)
- Hematocrit correction (Fahraeus) = (1 − Hct_LV) / (1 − Hct_SV) ≈ 0.733
