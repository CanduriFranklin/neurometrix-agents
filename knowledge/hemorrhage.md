# Intracranial Hemorrhage — Classification

## Types by location
- **Intraparenchymal (IPH)**: within brain parenchyma
- **Intraventricular (IVH)**: within ventricles
- **Subarachnoid (SAH)**: subarachnoid space
- **Subdural (SDH)**: between dura and arachnoid (crescent shape)
- **Epidural (EDH)**: between skull and dura (lentiform shape)

## CT density (HU)
- Hyperacute (< 6h): 40 – 60 HU
- Acute (6h – 3d): 60 – 80 HU, **hyperdense** typical
- Subacute (3d – 2w): 20 – 40 HU, isodense
- Chronic (> 2w): < 20 HU, hypodense (CSF-like)
- Source: Parizel 2001, Bradley 2007

## Indirect signs
- Midline shift > 5 mm
- Sulci and cistern effacement
- Hydrocephalus in IVH
- Midline shift + mass effect

## Size and risk
- Hematoma > 30 mL: surgical risk
- Hematoma > 60 mL: 30-day mortality > 30%
- Source: Broderick 1993, Hemphill 2015 (ICH score)

## ICH Score (Hemphill 2015)
- GCS (3-15)
- Hematoma volume (> 30 mL = 1 point)
- IVH (yes = 1)
- Infratentorial (yes = 1)
- Age > 80 (1 point)
Total: 0-6, 30-day mortality ranges from 0% to 100%
