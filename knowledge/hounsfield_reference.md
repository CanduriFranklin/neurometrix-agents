# Hounsfield Units — Reference

## Standard ranges (CT)

| Tissue | HU |
|---|---|
| Air | -1000 |
| Lung | -500 to -900 |
| Fat | -100 to -50 |
| Water / CSF | 0 ± 10 |
| White matter | 20 to 30 |
| Gray matter | 30 to 45 |
| Acute blood | 50 to 80 |
| Hyperacute blood | 40 to 60 |
| Iodinated contrast | 100 to 400 |
| Calcification | 100 to 400 |
| Cortical bone | 500 to 2000 |
| Metal / implants | > 2000 |

## Clinical windows (window / level)
- **Brain**: W=80, L=40
- **Blood**: W=100, L=50
- **Bone**: W=2000, L=500
- **Stroke**: W=8, L=32 (detects subtle hypodensity)
- **Subdural**: W=200, L=80

## Clinical applications
- Change < 5 HU between hemispheres: subclinical asymmetry
- Diffuse hypodensity (stroke): 1-3 HU reduction in gray matter
- MCA hyperdensity (cord sign): > 45 HU
- Normal pineal calcification: 100 to 400 HU
