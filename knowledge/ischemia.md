# Ischemic Stroke — Core, Penumbra, Mismatch

## Concept
- **Core**: infarcted tissue, unrecoverable
- **Penumbra**: hypoperfused but viable tissue, recoverable with reperfusion
- **Mismatch**: difference between penumbra and core

## CTP Detection (CT Perfusion)
- Core: TMax > 6s **AND** CBV < 30% of contralateral
- Penumbra: TMax > 6s **AND** preserved CBV (> 30% contralateral)
- Source: Campbell 2011 (EXTEND-IA), Lansberg 2012 (DEFUSE 2)

## Reperfusion criteria
- **DAWN trial** (Nogueira 2018): clinical-radiological mismatch up to 24h
- **DEFUSE 3** (Albers 2018): core < 70 mL, mismatch ratio > 1.8
- Patients with large penumbra (> 15 mL core, ratio > 1.8) → thrombectomy

## Therapeutic window
- < 4.5h: tPA (alteplase)
- 4.5 – 6h: tPA + thrombectomy
- 6 – 24h: thrombectomy if favorable mismatch

## Differential diagnosis
- Ischemic stroke vs hemorrhagic: non-contrast CT (hyperdensity in hemorrhage)
- Stroke vs tumor: CTP shows different mismatch, tumor has increased CBV
- Stroke vs migraine: reversible symptoms, normal CTP

## Poor prognosis markers
- ASPECTS < 6
- Large vessel occlusion (LVO) confirmed by CT angiography
- Core > 70 mL
- Mismatch ratio < 1.8
